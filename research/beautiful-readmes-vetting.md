  material, chooses the smallest justified scope and separates inspection,

- Two conditional references replace four longer overlapping references.
  Their 101 lines explain choices and counterexamples; they impose no layout.
  Equal correct outcomes with less reading cost support this simplification,
  not a claim of general superiority.

  cleanup, exact version boundaries and claims broader than implementation.
  The port audits independently found these reader failures; port-specific

- Overlapping reference material, a runtime helper framework, raw trial output
