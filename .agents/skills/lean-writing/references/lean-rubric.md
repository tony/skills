# Lean rubric

What `lean-writing` and the `lean-tighten` skill preserve while cutting. The
patterns to cut live in `prose-rules.md`.

## Preserve, do not strip

- Links, citations, anchors, and warnings that still substantiate a
  claim.
- Comments that pass the Keep list in `comment-policy.md`.
- Exact counts, dates, and SHAs used as evidence (benchmarks, release
  notes, stack traces, lockfiles).

When unsure, leave the text in place and ask.

## Replacements

A replacement is concrete and shorter than the original. Never narrate
your own edit inside the artifact.
